# ZAM 0.58.0: Six more school subjects from the ZAM Academy

Six more ZAM Academy apps reach ZAM: Mathematik 7, 8 and 10, Deutsch 9,
Wirtschaft und Recht 9 and Physik 8 for the Bavarian Realschule. Together
they bring 278 lessons and 876 cards, each linked to its lesson. Mathematics
now has an Academy cell for every year from 7 to 10.

## The ZAM Academy

- **Mathematik 7 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/mathematik-realschule-7](https://academy.zam-os.org/mathematik-realschule-7/)
  brings 42 lessons and 126 cards for Zweig I and II/III, with labs and
  tasks at two levels. It covers eight Lernbereiche in I and six in II/III.
  The earlier school cells stay available. Their 30 cards keep their
  learning history; all Academy cards start fresh, since no history
  transfer has been approved for this subject yet.
- **Mathematik 8 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/mathematik-realschule-8](https://academy.zam-os.org/mathematik-realschule-8/)
  brings 41 lessons and 141 cards for both branches, from geometry through
  equations and functions to data and chance. It replaces three earlier
  school cells: 17 of their 24 cards move to successors, while the other
  seven keep their history and stay active.
- **Mathematik 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/mathematik-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/mathematik-realschule-10/)
  brings 45 lessons and 148 cards for both branches, with trigonometry,
  vectors, powers, exponential and logarithmic functions, geometry and
  statistics. All 16 cards from the two earlier school cells move to
  successors that preserve the questions and answers they were learned on.
- **Deutsch 9 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/deutsch-realschule-9](https://academy.zam-os.org/deutsch-realschule-9/)
  brings 53 lessons and 159 cards across all four Lernbereiche, for every
  Wahlpflichtfächergruppe. It replaces the earlier argumentation cell.
  Its eight old cards leave active reviews and retain their history in
  your library; the new questions start fresh. Lessons appear under the
  existing Deutsch areas in the atlas, with a new area for speaking and
  listening.
- **Wirtschaft und Recht 9 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/wirtschaft-recht-realschule-9](https://academy.zam-os.org/wirtschaft-recht-realschule-9/)
  brings 46 core lessons, four optional English lessons and 150 cards for
  groups I and III. It covers careers, the social market economy, consumer
  protection and law. It replaces the earlier partial cell: three of its
  eight cards move to successors; five leave active reviews and retain
  their history in your library.
- **Physik 8 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/physik-realschule-8](https://academy.zam-os.org/physik-realschule-8/)
  brings 47 lessons and 152 cards, with the separate curriculum paths for
  Zweig I and II/III and optional acoustics and astronomy. It replaces two
  earlier school cells: four of their 16 cards move to successors, ten
  leave active reviews and retain their history, and the two Ohm's law
  cards stay active unchanged.
- **Your history moves only with an approved mapping.** The 40 transfers in
  this release follow explicit decisions reviewed card by card in
  zam-os/zam-academy. Taking a new cell applies its mapping. ZAM never
  transfers mastery on a guess or combines two reviewed histories.

## Curriculum and maintenance

- **Wirtschaft und Recht 9** is now a LehrplanPLUS curriculum position for
  groups I, IIIa and IIIb, with its four required Lernbereiche and optional
  bilingual offer. It is not offered to group II.
- **Physik 8 curriculum links** now open the current Fachlehrplan pages for
  both branches.
- **Academy sync works on Windows.** The release tooling now starts its
  formatter directly, fixing a failure after new cells had been copied.

## Worth knowing

- **No schema change.**
- **Bundled cells:** 18 Academy cells now. Nine earlier school cells leave
  the bundle: three Mathematik 8, two Mathematik 10, two Physik 8, one
  Deutsch 9 and one Wirtschaft und Recht 9. The Mathematik 7 school cells
  remain bundled.
- **Design notes:** ADR 2026-10-19 (ZAM Academy cells), ADR 2026-08-14
  Decisions 9–10 (explicit successors, cells before generic import).
