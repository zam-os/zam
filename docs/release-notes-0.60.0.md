# ZAM 0.60.0: Seven more Academy subjects

Geschichte, Deutsch, Englisch, BwR, Politik und Gesellschaft and
Französisch for grade 10 and Biologie 8 for the Bavarian Realschule now
ship with ZAM. Together they bring 286 lessons and 682 cards, each linked
to its lesson in the ZAM Academy.

## The ZAM Academy

- **Geschichte 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/geschichte-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/geschichte-realschule-10/)
  brings 53 lessons (four of them optional English lessons) and 159 cards
  for every Wahlpflichtfächergruppe. It replaces the earlier Cold War
  school cell: two of its cards move with their whole review histories to
  approved successors; the two multi-part questions leave active reviews
  and keep their history.
- **Deutsch 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/deutsch-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/deutsch-realschule-10/)
  brings 30 lessons and 60 cards on literature, media, grammar and
  spelling for every group. The four earlier Deutsch 10 cards stay
  active until a mapping is decided.
- **Englisch 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/englisch-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/englisch-realschule-10/)
  brings 46 lessons and 92 cards for every group. The four earlier
  Englisch 10 cards stay active.
- **BwR 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/bwr-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/bwr-realschule-10/)
  brings 38 lessons and 76 cards for Wahlpflichtfächergruppe II. It
  replaces the earlier calculation school cell: two cards move with their
  histories to approved successors; the two questions that asked for a
  whole calculation scheme leave active reviews and keep their history.
- **Politik und Gesellschaft 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/politik-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/politik-realschule-10/)
  brings 32 lessons and 64 cards across methods, participation,
  structures and action fields, and gets its own subject with four areas
  in the knowledge atlas. If you already took up the earlier version, ZAM
  corrects its curriculum links on the next catalog update without
  touching your cards or reviews.
- **Französisch 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/franzoesisch-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/franzoesisch-realschule-10/)
  brings 57 lessons and 171 French cards for Wahlpflichtfächergruppe
  IIIa. It replaces no earlier cell.
- **Biologie 8 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/biologie-realschule-8](https://academy.zam-os.org/biologie-realschule-8/)
  brings 30 lessons and 60 cards on methods, microorganisms, the immune
  system, development and evolution for every group. Microorganisms get
  their own area in the knowledge atlas. The two earlier Biologie 8
  school cells stay active.

## Taking up the new content

Open **Aus der ZAM Academy** in Studio or Mobile and choose **Aufnehmen**
for the cell you want to learn. Installing the app update makes the
bundled content available; taking up a cell creates your cards and applies
its approved mapping. Repeating the action creates no duplicate cards or
review histories.

History moves only through explicit, reviewed successor declarations.
The four transfers in this release follow the approved mappings from the
Academy. Four earlier cards leave active reviews without transferring
mastery. ZAM keeps their review evidence and never infers a match from
similar wording.

## For agents

- **Worktrees of a trusted project work.** When an agent works in a new
  Git worktree of a project you already allowed, ZAM now accepts that
  worktree's files, for example when it writes a knowledge article. Hidden
  files and links that lead outside the allowed folders stay blocked. An
  agent that is already connected needs one restart after the update.

## Worth knowing

- **No schema change.**
- **29 Academy cells are now bundled.** Two earlier school cells leave
  the bundle: Geschichte 10 and BwR 10.
- **Design notes:** ADR 2026-10-19 (ZAM Academy cells), ADR 2026-10-26
  (curriculum binding withdrawals), ADR 2026-10-26 (registered worktree
  paths), ADR 2026-08-14 Decisions 9–10 (explicit successors, cells before
  generic import).
