# ZAM 0.62.0: Mathematik for the Gymnasium, and grades 5 and 6

Nine more ZAM Academy subjects now ship with ZAM. Five of them are the first
for the Bavarian Gymnasium: Mathematik in grades 5, 6, 11, 12 and 13. Three
open grades 5 and 6 at the Realschule, and Geographie 8 joins grade 8.

## Taking up the new content

Open **Aus der ZAM Academy** in Studio or Mobile and choose **Aufnehmen** for
the cell you want to learn. You can also choose your grade in the curriculum
offer; the Academy cell now appears there beside the earlier cards of that
grade. Taking up a cell creates your cards and applies its approved mapping.
Repeating it creates no duplicate cards or review histories.

A review history moves only to a successor that asks for exactly the same
thing. Thomas approved each of these mappings. In this release, 38 histories
move this way. Every other earlier card keeps its history and stays in your
reviews. Where you already know a card that moved, the new cards of the same
lesson arrive one at a time, not all at once.

## Worth knowing

- **No schema change, no code change.** This release only adds content.
- **47 Academy cells are now bundled.** No school cell leaves the bundle in
  this release.
- **Design notes:** ADR 2026-10-19 (ZAM Academy cells), ADR 2026-08-14
  Decisions 9–10 (explicit successors, cells before generic import).
