# ZAM 0.51.0 — A calmer Studio, and your learning content in one place

The desktop Studio has a new look and a simpler layout. A sidebar takes you
where you want to go, Today shows one clear next step, and a learning
session shows nothing but the card. Learning Content now separates the cards
you have from the ones you could take, and shows where content comes from.

## Today and learning

- **A sidebar.** Today, Learning Content, Knowledge graph and Statistics sit
  on the left; Settings, the AI status and the language sit at the bottom.
  In a narrow window the sidebar becomes a bar across the top.
- **Today.** How many cards are due, about how long they take at your own
  pace, the topics they come from, and one button: **Start learning**. Below
  it, the reviews of your last seven days.
- **Focused sessions.** While you learn, the sidebar steps aside. A bar at
  the top shows your progress, the learning mode and a ⋯ menu to edit, open
  or remove the card.
- **A clearer card.** After the reveal, your answer and the reference answer
  sit side by side. **Again** stands apart from Hard, Good and Easy, and the
  rating buttons stay in reach while you scroll through long feedback.
- **A calmer look.** Cool grey and white with one blue accent, in fonts made
  for reading. The fonts come with the app and work offline. Dark mode
  follows the same design.

## Learning Content

- **Three lists.** **Personal** holds the cards you have. **Not chosen**
  holds published items you have no card for yet, grouped by where they come
  from. **Unpublished** holds drafts and items still in review.
- **One Import menu.** Photo or PDF, library topics, goal import and file
  import sit in one menu next to **New card**.
- **Goal import any time.** Goal import opens in its own window, not only
  during first setup. A goal you have started is still there when you come
  back.
- **Sources.** Pick one source at a time: a workspace, a folder, or the
  curriculum. A repository shows its knowledge articles and its knowledge
  map across the full width, and you switch the map's view right on the
  map. Articles and cited files open in a reader with formatted text.
- **Clearer architecture diagrams.** In the C4 view, arrows go around boxes
  instead of hiding behind them, and their labels no longer cover a box.
- **The curriculum has one way in:** Sources → Curriculum.

## Settings

- **Sections at a glance.** A list beside the settings jumps to each section
  and follows as you scroll.
- **Choices you can see.** Learning mode, study plan and theme are cards and
  buttons instead of drop-down lists, and the study plan is saved as you
  change it.
- **No switch for the knowledge map.** The map shows in Sources without
  turning it on first.

## Languages

- Spanish, French, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese show about a dozen new
  labels in English until they have been reviewed.
