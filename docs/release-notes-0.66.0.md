# ZAM 0.66.0 Release Notes

ZAM 0.66.0 bundles ten more Academy courses, bringing the total to 83. It also includes fixes for editor selection and paused study sessions.

Fourteen superseded school cells move to the legacy snapshot directory. The archive includes Grade 5 German and Grade 6 Biology, English and History in Gymnasium, as well as Grade 6 German, English, Geography and History and Grade 7 Physics in Realschule. Existing learners keep their review evidence. The nine approved mappings transfer 33 matching histories to their Academy successors when learners take the new cells. Existing keep-active cards retain their histories. The Grade 7 German syntax, English and Geography school cells remain available at their own grade.

## Study sessions

- Editor selection and resumption of paused sessions are more reliable (#488).
- Studio, Desktop and Mobile keep pause, resume and session state in step (#489).
