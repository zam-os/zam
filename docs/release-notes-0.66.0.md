# ZAM 0.66.0 Release Notes

ZAM 0.66.0 bundles five more Academy courses for Grade 6, bringing the total to 78. It also includes fixes for editor selection and paused study sessions.

## ZAM Academy

| Course | School type | Lessons | Review items |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| German 6 | Gymnasium | 49 | 98 |
| English 6 | Gymnasium | 40 | 120 |
| Geography 6 | Realschule | 25 | 50 |
| History 6 | Gymnasium | 36 | 72 |
| History 6 | Realschule | 42 | 126 |

Five superseded Grade 6 school cells move to the legacy snapshot directory: English and two History cells for Gymnasium, and Geography and History for Realschule. Existing learners keep their review evidence. The four approved mappings transfer 18 matching cards to Academy successors when learners take the new cells. The German Grade 7 syntax cell and the Geography Grade 7 school cell remain available at their own grade.

## Study sessions

- Editor selection and resumption of paused sessions are more reliable (#488).
- Studio, Desktop and Mobile keep pause, resume and session state in step (#489).
