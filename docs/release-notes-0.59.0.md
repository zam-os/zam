# ZAM 0.59.0: Informatik 10 and three more Academy subjects

Informatik 10, Chemie 10, Englisch 9 and Französisch 9 for the Bavarian
Realschule now ship with ZAM. Together they bring 172 lessons and 420 cards,
each linked to its lesson in the ZAM Academy.

## The ZAM Academy

- **Informatik 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/informatik-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/informatik-realschule-10/)
  brings 36 lessons and 72 cards across Datennetze II, objektorientierte
  Softwareentwicklung, logische Schaltungen, Robotik und eingebettete
  Systeme, and künstliche Intelligenz. These are elective IT modules:
  the school chooses when to teach them. The cell carries no fixed grade
  or branch claim, and the general curriculum importer stays available
  where only modular coverage fits. Existing Algorithmen, Datennetze, SQL
  and UML cards keep their learning history. All new Informatik cards
  start fresh when you take up the cell.
- **Chemie 10 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/chemie-realschule-10](https://academy.zam-os.org/chemie-realschule-10/)
  brings 40 lessons and 100 cards, with separate curriculum bindings for
  Zweig I and II/III and their elective areas. It replaces two earlier
  partial school cells. Seven of their 16 cards move to approved
  successors; the other nine keep their history and stay active in the
  libraries that hold them.
- **Englisch 9 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/englisch-realschule-9](https://academy.zam-os.org/englisch-realschule-9/)
  brings 36 core lessons, four optional grammar refreshers and 80 English
  cards for every Wahlpflichtfächergruppe. It replaces the earlier
  eight-card grammar cell: three questions move with their whole review
  histories to approved successors; five leave active reviews and retain
  their history in your library.
- **Französisch 9 Realschule.**
  [academy.zam-os.org/franzoesisch-realschule-9](https://academy.zam-os.org/franzoesisch-realschule-9/)
  brings 56 lessons and 168 French cards for Wahlpflichtfächergruppe IIIa.
  It replaces the earlier four-card partial cell. All four old cards
  leave active reviews and retain their history; the new questions start
  fresh.

## Taking up the new content

Open **Aus der ZAM Academy** in Studio or Mobile and choose **Aufnehmen**
for the cell you want to learn. Installing the app update makes the
bundled content available; taking up a cell creates your cards and applies
its approved mapping. Repeating the action creates no duplicate cards or
review histories.

History moves only through explicit, reviewed successor declarations.
The ten transfers in this release follow the approved mappings from the
Academy. Nine earlier cards leave active reviews without transferring
mastery. ZAM keeps their review evidence and never infers a match from
similar wording.

## Worth knowing

- **No schema change.**
- **22 Academy cells are now bundled.** Four earlier partial school cells
  leave the bundle: one Englisch 9, one Französisch 9 and two Chemie 10.
  Informatik 10 replaces no earlier cell.
- **Design notes:** ADR 2026-10-19 (ZAM Academy cells), ADR 2026-08-14
  Decisions 9–10 (explicit successors, cells before generic import).
